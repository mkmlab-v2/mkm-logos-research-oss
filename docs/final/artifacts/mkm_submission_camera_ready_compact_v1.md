# MKM Camera-Ready Compact (v1)

## Title

Two-Track Safety-Gated Inference for Risk-Control Decision Support

## Compact Abstract

We present a two-track safety-gated inference framework that separates a knowledge lane (K-track) from an operational survivorship lane (T-track). The design prevents direct promotion of expressive but potentially overfit signals into operations without explicit gate passage. Evaluation is artifact-anchored and reproducible through baseline comparison, raw OOS readiness checks, bootstrap/permutation significance testing, falsification suite validation, and first-break boundary disclosure. Product surfaces are intentionally split: Macro Risk Warning API for risk-off governance in operational contexts, and Logos Resonance Explorer API for research-grade corpus bridge analysis, including Aramaic-inclusive pipelines. Research outputs do not auto-promote to operational triggers. The system is positioned for risk-control observability and governance, not directional return guarantees.

## Camera-Ready Claims (Allowed)

- Claims are tied to as-of artifacts with reproducible paths.
- Operational and exploratory lanes are explicitly separated.
- Failure boundaries are disclosed, not hidden.
- Risk-off governance is prioritized over directional alpha claims.

## Camera-Ready Claims (Disallowed)

- "Guaranteed return", "certain direction", or equivalent wording.
- Treating roadmap labels (e.g., 12AI) as fully active production runtime.
- Presenting `research_only` findings as production-validated outcomes.
- Mixing B-track exploratory evidence into A-track operational claims without gate evidence.

## Required Disclosure Block

All quantitative values are as-of artifact snapshots and may vary across runs and versions.  
This system is intended for risk-control and governance observability, not investment advice or guaranteed directional outcomes.  
A-track operational evidence and B-track exploratory evidence are disclosed separately by design.
