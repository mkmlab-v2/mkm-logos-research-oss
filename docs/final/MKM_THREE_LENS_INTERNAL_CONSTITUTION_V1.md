# MKM Three-Lens Internal Constitution v1

Date: 2026-05-06  
Status: INTERNAL_LOCKED_DRAFT

## 1) Purpose

This document locks MKM's internal interpretation standard for the three lenses:

- `myeongni` (mid-term direction core)
- `logos` (macro/meaning gate, non-execution)
- `sasang` (short-term intensity and stress modulation)

It is an internal operating constitution for reproducible interpretation and conflict arbitration.  
Implementation facts, paths, and gate status remain controlled by `CONSTITUTION_INFERENCE_IMPLEMENTATION_FACTS.md`.

## 2) Core Principles

1. **Fact-Lock first**: no implementation/performance claims without script/artifact/exit-code evidence.
2. **Lane firewall**: no automatic bridge from research outputs to live execution triggers.
3. **Deterministic arbitration**: lens conflicts must converge via policy rules, not narrative preference.
4. **Action contract**: final output must be mapped to `GO/WATCH/HOLD` plus trigger conditions.
5. **No absolute claims**: no deterministic fate, no medical diagnosis/treatment claims, no guaranteed returns.

## 3) Lens Roles (Fixed Contract)

### 3.1 Myeongni Lens

- Role: mid-term direction model.
- Signals: strength/balance, ten-god composition, hidden-stem pressure, school disagreement.
- Output: directional tendency + confidence + risk friction.
- Constraint: no direct buy/sell order text.

### 3.2 Logos Lens

- Role: macro meaning/risk gate (`[NON_GATING]` in direct execution sense).
- Signals: regime resonance, semantic stress, context coherence.
- Output: macro narrative pressure and scenario guardrails.
- Constraint: no direct execution trigger by logos-only signal.

### 3.3 Sasang Lens

- Role: short-term intensity and stress transition monitor.
- Signals: exchange imbalance, constitution-mode pressure, phase transition tendency.
- Output: volatility/stress modulation and posture adjustment hints.
- Constraint: no medical diagnosis or treatment language.

## 4) Conflict Arbitration Policy

When lens outputs conflict, apply the following order:

1. **Safety gate precedence**: hard risk gate wins over directional optimism.
2. **Regime alignment**: macro regime mismatch downgrades confidence.
3. **Disagreement penalty**: high school disagreement forces conservative posture.
4. **Depth-over-surface rule**: hidden structural pressure can veto surface momentum.
5. **Final convergence**: one final `GO/WATCH/HOLD` only.

If convergence confidence is below threshold, default to `WATCH`.

## 5) Output Contract (Internal)

All internal/partner-facing summaries should follow:

1. `Field` (regime context)
2. `Lens` (myeongni/logos/sasang outputs)
3. `Conflict` (why and how it was resolved)
4. `Final Action` (`GO/WATCH/HOLD`)
5. `Trigger Conditions` (upgrade/downgrade conditions)
6. `Disclaimer` (non-medical, non-investment-advice, non-legal-advice)

## 6) Language Guardrail

- Preferred: risk band, scenario, confidence interval, posture, trigger.
- Forbidden: guaranteed success/failure, absolute fate, diagnosis/treatment wording, exaggerated superiority claims.
- Mandatory footer:
  - `This output is decision-support and wellness-oriented guidance, not medical diagnosis, investment advice, or legal advice.`

## 7) Governance Note

This constitution standardizes interpretation behavior and messaging discipline.  
It does **not** replace legal review requirements or external compliance validation.
