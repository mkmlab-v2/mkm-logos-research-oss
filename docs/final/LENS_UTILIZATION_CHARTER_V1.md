> **Final Action = Field (`regime_map` + Science/ops gates)만.**  
> **3렌즈 = 해석·교육·관측 사이드바 (`[NON_GATING]`).**

`Field observation → parallel slices (사상·명리·성경·Science@{domain}) → Conflict surface → Advisory only`
