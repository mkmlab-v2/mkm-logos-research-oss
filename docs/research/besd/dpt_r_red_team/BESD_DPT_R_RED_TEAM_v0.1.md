## 3. PRIORITY ATTACK 1 — AGENCY_SOVEREIGNTY_TO_AUTONOMISM

### Attack thesis
Agency Sovereignty collapses into generic autonomy / liberal self-rule / psychological reactance (“restore freedom when threatened”).

### Adversarial probes

| Probe | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Telos misperceived → agent “authors” harmful act | **LEAK risk** if Truth/Context in π not operationalized (ABSTAIN missing) |
| Dignity used to rationalize revenge | **BLOCKED only if** Mimetic Break + Dignity are hard; Agency alone fails |
| Competing authorities / religious certainty overrides evidence | **GAP** — Sovereignty without Truth constraint = autonomism |
| Reactance theory subsumption | **HIGH overlap** — “reclaim agency under coercion” ≈ freedom restoration |

### Construct cut
- **Agency Sovereignty as meta-label** largely **re-describes** Role Refusal + chosen non-mimetic action.
- Removing Agency Sovereignty while keeping Role Refusal + Dignity + Non-Mimetic **does not collapse** Matthew-5-style fixture function → **independence WEAK**.
- Residual non-autonomist content (if any) lives in **π(Truth, Context)** + third-party harm — **not yet operational in stub**.

### Attack outcome
**PARTIAL_SUCCESS (narrowing).** Agency Sovereignty **omitted from minimal distinctive kernel**; retained only as **assistive meta-description** with required future safeguards (Truth operationalization, ABSTAIN, third-party harm). Does **not** alone force FAIL.
